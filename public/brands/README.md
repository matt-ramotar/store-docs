# Agent brand artwork

These marks identify the corresponding products in Store6's agent actions. They remain the
property of their respective owners and do not imply sponsorship or endorsement. The artwork
is separate from the Store6 skill's Apache-2.0 license.

Retrieved 2026-09-08. SVG geometry, view boxes, and source-file colors are preserved. The Claude
badge displays its mark in white on clay orange through CSS brightness/inversion filters. The
OpenAI badge displays its mark in black on white. Menu entries retain their source colors.
