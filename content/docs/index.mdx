Store reads and writes data that lives in more than one place: a network, a local database, and
memory. Store 6 is the next major line; Store 5 continues under its own coordinates for the whole
6.x major.

## Why Store?

Reading data across those boundaries means coordinating freshness, failures, deduplicated fetches,
and cache bounds by hand around every endpoint. Store gives your application one typed contract for
those reads and writes on every Kotlin platform, so that coordination is written once and tested
once.
