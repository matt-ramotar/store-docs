The resulting bounded statement is therefore: with Store 6's default freshness
validator and `Freshness.CachedOrFetch`, a first cold stream after restart can
serve a durably invalidated persisted row as
`Data(origin=Origin.SOT, isStale=true, refreshing=true)`. A failed refresh emits
`Error(StoreError.Fetch, servedStale=true)` without an intervening `Loading`;
bounded queued stale `Data` replays are permitted, and the stream remains live.
Missing metadata is a separate default revalidation cause. After the first
hydrated emission, later resident delivery may carry `Origin.MEMORY`.
